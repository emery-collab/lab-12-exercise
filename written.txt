Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
The bounding boxes update to contain the circles at their new positions, but the BVH's original tree structure 
remains unchanged. As circles move, bounding boxes can become larger and overlap more.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
The BVH is rebuilt using the circles' current positions. This reorganizes the tree and produces tighter, more 
efficient bounding boxes, but requires more computation than refitting.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
The circles are drawn with their sides cut off because the BVH is rebuilt before updating the scene. 
This causes the BVH bounding boxes to represent the circles' previous positions rather than their 
current positions. During rendering, the intersection algorithm uses these outdated bounding boxes 
to prune unnecessary intersection tests. Consequently, pixels belonging to portions of circles outside 
their old bounding boxes are incorrectly discarded. Updating the scene before rebuilding the BVH fixes the issue.
